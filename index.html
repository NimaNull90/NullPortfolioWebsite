About Me

My name is Nima Bagheri. I am a third-year animator at SFU's Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT) program. My interests include game development and animation, including flash animation, CGI and pixel art.

I have animation skills in Adobe Animate, Blender, Maya and Aseprite, with experience in both 2D and 3D animation pipelines.
