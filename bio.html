About Me

My name is Nima Bagheri. I am a third-year animator at SFU's Interactive Arts and Technology (SIAT) program. My interests include game development and animation, including flash animation, CGI and pixel art. My desire in creating animations started back in 2019 when I started analyzing animations I'd watch in my free time.

I enrolled in SIAT with the initial purpose of an animation program. While I achieved it through the IAT343 course, I ended up applying this skill to my projects in other courses such as IAT312. I have animation skills in Adobe Animate, Blender, Maya and Aseprite.

Skills & Software

  • 3D Animation: Autodesk Maya, Blender
  • 2D Animation: Adobe Animate, Adobe After Effects
  • Pixel Art: Aseprite
  • Technical Skills: Character rigging, UV mapping, weight painting
  • Animation Principles: 12 principles of animation, timing, spacing, appeal

Experience & Expertise

  • Lead technical animator on fully animated film project
  • Character rigging specialist with focus on non-humanoid characters
  • Proficient in both 2D and 3D animation pipelines
  • Experience with facial rigging and expression systems
  • Collaborative team member with strong communication skills

Animation Philosophy

I believe that animation is all about movement with style coming in as secondary. A good animation can look lackluster in style and a stylistic media can be lacking in good animation. Depending on the type of animation, it can be somewhat easy or generally hard and time consuming as it isn't merely "just art". With proper technical knowledge and artistic capatbilities, animations can be fantastic to make.
