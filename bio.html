About Me

I'm a multi-medium animator and artist with expertise in various mediums of Flash animation, CGI and pixel art. I graduated in 2026 from Simon Fraser University's Interactive Arts & Technology (IAT) program with a concentration in Extended Reality & Game Design, having worked on various projects ranging from short films to interactive experiences such as video games.

I enrolled in IAT with the initial purpose of an animation program. Although the IAT343 course was the only one dedicated to animation, I ended up applying this skill to my projects in other courses such as IAT312.

I also like to extract assets from video games to see how animations are implemented, allowing me to learn new techniques and find the link between animation and programming within a video game, which is always fun to see how it's done :D
